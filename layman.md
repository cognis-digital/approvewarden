When you use a decentralized app (like a token swap or NFT marketplace), you often grant it permission to move your crypto tokens on your behalf — these are called "approvals." Approvewarden lets you check any wallet's outstanding approvals to see if any are dangerously broad or granted to suspicious addresses. It assigns each approval a risk score and tells you exactly which ones to revoke. It is designed for crypto users, security teams, and developers who want to audit wallet safety from the command line or as part of an automated pipeline.
